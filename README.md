# Projector Pilot

**The premium controller for Epson and PJLink projectors** - power, the full picture menu, motorized lens shift, focus and zoom, one-tap Scenes, and real home automation, straight from your phone, tablet or desktop.

Website and full user guide: **https://projectorpilot.com**

- No ads. No subscription. No cloud account. One-time purchase, unlocked on every platform you own.
- 29 languages. Android, iOS, Windows and macOS.
- Talks to your projector over ESC/VP21, PJLink and Epson Web Control, using whichever answers.

## What it does

- **Dashboard** - live status, one-tap power and input, customizable cards.
- **Remote** - navigation D-pad, inputs, screen blank, and geometry correction.
- **Picture** - the full menu on the projector's own scale, read back after every change.
- **Lens** - motorized shift, focus, zoom and memories, with a step size you choose.
- **Scenes** - save the whole room and apply it in one tap; every Scene becomes a Home Assistant script.
- **Home automation** - Home Assistant, Hubitat and webhooks.
