What each costs

Simple, one-time prices

Free

The essentials, forever. Add a projector, power it on and off, switch inputs, watch live status, and use the on-screen remote basics. No trial clock, no nag.

Pro

$9.99, one-time. Scenes, multiple projectors, full picture control, lens and geometry, maintenance and insights, home-screen widgets, test patterns and shareable scene profiles.

Home Automation

$2.99, one-time add-on. Home Assistant, Hubitat and webhooks. Read the setup guide to see exactly what it does.

Bundle

$11.99, one-time: Pro + Home Automation, at a saving. Everything the app does, in one purchase, cheaper than buying the two halves apart.

Prices shown are the store's. Whatever the app displays is the figure your store quoted, in your own currency, before you confirm. The prices above are the US reference prices; your region may differ, and the app never invents a number.
The fine print, minus the fine

How buying works

  • One-time purchase. There is no subscription. You pay once and it is yours.
  • No ads, no account, no cloud. The app talks to your projector on your network. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing sold on the side.
  • One purchase unlocks every platform you own. Buy on one store and restore on the others tied to the same account - Android, iOS, Windows and macOS.
  • Buying or restoring something you already own always unlocks it. If the store says you already own Pro, Home Automation or the bundle, the app grants it rather than throwing an error, so a feature you paid for can never lock you out.
  • On a platform with no store, the app includes everything rather than showing a paywall nobody can pay.
