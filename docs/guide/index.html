Projector Pilot User Guide

Everything you need to add your projector and use every screen - the dashboard, the deep remote, the full picture menu, motorized lens, Scenes and real home automation. Every screen adapts to your hardware: the app only shows the controls your projector actually exposes, and every value is read back from the projector, never guessed. So your screens may show fewer options than a page lists here, and that is the app being honest, not broken.

Browse the guide

Every screen, explained

Start with Getting started, then dive into any screen. Nothing here needs a cloud account - Projector Pilot talks to your projector on your own network over ESC/VP21, PJLink and Epson Web Control, using whichever answers.

New to Projector Pilot?

Three screens do most of the work.

🚀

Add your projector

Scan your network or add by IP, run the one-time capability scan, Test Connection and Save. On the same Wi-Fi in about a minute.
📊

Read the dashboard

Live power, input, color mode and projection info, with cards you add, hide and reorder to build the view you want.
🎭

Save a Scene

Capture power, input, picture, lens and more, then recall the whole room in one tap - and hand it to Home Assistant.
