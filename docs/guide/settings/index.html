Settings

Make it yours

Settings is where you manage your projectors, unlock the app, theme it, tune how it polls, and back the whole thing up. It was reorganised in the latest release so device controls, purchases, appearance and links each sit in one clear place. No ads, no subscription, no cloud account - just the switches that matter.

Projectors

Add, edit and connect

  • Add a projector two ways. Scan your Wi-Fi or wired network for anything that answers a projector and let the app fill in the address, or type an IP by hand. A newly added projector shows a progress bar while its capabilities are scanned, and the result is remembered so opening the app again does not re-scan it.
  • Edit, inspect or delete. Each saved projector has an edit pencil, an advanced connection view, an info panel and a delete. Advanced is where the per-projector ports live, alongside the three passwords.
  • Three separate passwords. A projector has up to three: Web Control, PJLink and Remote. They are different passwords for different protocols, saved per projector, and the app works out which one a projector actually wants rather than assuming it is protected.
Unlocking the app

Pro, Home Automation, and the bundle

Projector Pilot is sold in two halves as one-time purchases. Pro is the advanced app; Home Automation is its own purchase; a bundle buys both. Anyone who bought Pro before the split keeps everything Pro had.

  • Projector Pilot Pro - Scenes, multiple projectors, full picture control, lens and geometry, maintenance, widgets, test patterns and shareable profiles.
  • Home Automation - Home Assistant, Hubitat and webhooks.
  • Bundle - both, in one purchase.
  • Restore - re-grants what you already bought on this store account. If the store reports an item as already owned, the app grants it instead of showing an error, so a feature you paid for can never lock you out.
Prices are always the Store's, shown in your own currency before you confirm - the app never invents a figure. On a platform with no store, the app includes everything rather than showing a paywall nobody can pay. See the full breakdown on Pricing.

Common questions

Settings FAQ

How do I add a projector?
Settings, Add Projector. Either let the app scan your network and fill in the address, or type the IP by hand. A new projector is scanned once for its capabilities, with a progress bar, and the result is remembered.
Where are the ports and passwords?
Open a saved projector's advanced connection view. That is where the per-projector ports and the three separate passwords - Web Control, PJLink and Remote - live.
I already bought Pro - do I have to buy Home Automation?
No. Anyone who bought Pro before the app was split into two halves keeps everything Pro had. Nothing that was unlocked is taken away.
Do my passwords end up in a backup?
No. Backup and Restore covers your app settings; saved projectors and their passwords stay in encrypted secure storage and never travel in a backup file.
Can I change how often it polls the projector?
Yes - Status Polling Interval sets how often the live state refreshes, and the change takes effect immediately without a burst of queries while you drag the slider.
