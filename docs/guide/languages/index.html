One projector app, 29 languages

Every screen, every setting, every dialog - in your language. Projector Pilot speaks English plus 28 more, including the permission prompts on iPhone, iPad and Mac, follows your device out of the box, and lets you pick one yourself in Settings. Arabic and Hebrew mirror the whole layout, right to left.

Where it lives

Settings › Language

Out of the box the app follows your device language. To choose one yourself, open Settings and tap Language. System default hands the choice back to the device; below it every language is listed under its own name, so you can find yours without reading another one first.

  • Automatic. Match the phone or tablet with nothing to set.
  • Your pick. Override the device and the choice applies at once and survives a restart.
  • Right to left. Arabic and Hebrew turn the whole layout around - navigation, cards and lists mirror - while the values read off the projector still read the way the projector writes them.
The projector's own words stay the projector's. Input names, color modes and every value the projector reports are shown exactly as it says them, in any language. Only Projector Pilot's own copy is translated.

Common questions

Languages FAQ

Does the app follow my device language automatically?
Yes. Out of the box it follows the device. To pick one yourself, open Settings › Language; System default hands the choice back to the device.
Are Arabic and Hebrew laid out right to left?
Yes. The whole app mirrors - navigation, cards and lists - while the values read off the projector still read the way the projector writes them.
Why are my input names still in their original language?
Because they are the projector's own words, not the app's. Input names, color modes and every value the projector reports are shown exactly as it says them. Only Projector Pilot's own copy is translated.
