What it does

Every control your projector exposes, in one clean app.

Projector Pilot talks to your projector over ESC/VP21, PJLink and Epson Web Control at once, using whichever answers - so you get power, picture, geometry and lens control that actually works, with values read back from the projector, never guessed.

Dashboard

Everything at a glance, built the way you want it.

  • Live status - power, input, color mode, light-source hours, signal and picture figures, updated as they change.
  • One-tap power and input right from the top, with a warm-up and cool-down aware power pill.
  • Customizable cards - add, hide and reorder the cards so the dashboard shows what matters to you.
  • Projection info - dynamic range, color space, gamma, laser hours, serial and mount, read from the projector.
Remote & Picture

A deep remote and the full picture menu.

  • Full navigation D-pad, menu, source, and screen blank - the on-screen remote, done right.
  • Correction method - H/V keystone, Quick Corner, point correction, curved surface and corner wall.
  • Full picture control - brightness, contrast, tint, sharpness, color temperature, gamma, HDR and the deep picture menu, each on the projector's own scale.
  • HDR and color space are separate - a BT.2020 SDR signal reads as exactly that, never a false HDR badge.
Lens

Motorized lens shift, focus, zoom and memories.

  • Shift, focus and zoom from a precise pad, with a step size you choose - fine for framing, coarse for big moves.
  • Lens memories - recall and save the positions your projector supports.
  • Orientation-aware - the app follows the projector's own mapping, ceiling or rear, so up is always up.
  • Honest readouts - a position the projector cannot confirm is never dressed up as a lock.
Scenes

One tap for the whole room.

  • Save a Scene - power, input, picture, signal, light, geometry and audio, and pick exactly which parts to keep.
  • Apply in one tap - "Movie Night" sets the input, the picture mode and the lens memory together.
  • Every Scene becomes a Home Assistant script, so an automation or a voice assistant can run it too.
  • Shareable profiles - export a tuned Scene and hand it to a friend.
Home automation

Home Assistant, Hubitat and webhooks - the real thing.

  • Home Assistant - sign in, publish the projector's state as a sensor, and tap Sync With HA to get a script for every Scene and control.
  • A dashboard built for you - Sync builds it in Home Assistant, with built-in cards, upgraded to the richer HACS cards when you have them. It works with the core PJLink integration too.
  • A setup checklist shows what is done, what is missing and what to do next, and your readings stay in Home Assistant while the projector is off.
  • Hubitat - mirror the projector onto a device through Maker API so Rule Machine can act on it.
  • Webhooks - post to any URL when the projector powers on, powers off, changes input or recalls a Scene.

And more

The details that make it feel finished.

Switch projectors from anywhere

A projector switcher in the top bar of every projector page moves you between projectors without going back to the list.

Power-off asks first

Turning the projector off asks you to confirm, so a stray tap does not end the movie.

Controls that respond instantly

Lens shift, focus, zoom and the light-output stepper move the moment you press, and the app notices the projector turning on within about ten seconds.

Firmware at a glance

Your projector's firmware version shows on the Dashboard and the Info tab, and in the diagnostics report, whenever the projector reports it.

Input switching on PJLink-only projectors

Projectors that only list their inputs over PJLink, such as the TW7300 and Home Cinema 4040, switch input, and non-Epson PJLink projectors can too.

Find your projector on Wi-Fi

Add Projector scans your network for anything answering as a projector and fills in the address, with a progress bar while your model's capabilities are discovered.

Backup and restore

Export your app settings to a file and import them again. The file is shared under its real name, and saved projectors and passwords never travel in it.

Report a bug in one step

Report a Bug builds one bundle with every relevant log, ready to send.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is Projector Pilot made by Epson?

No. Projector Pilot is an independent app by MiyaraHub Technologies LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epson, the PJLink standards body, Home Assistant or Hubitat. Those names are trademarks of their respective owners and appear here only to describe which projectors and smart-home systems the app works with.

Which projectors does Projector Pilot support?

Epson projectors over ESC/VP21 and Web Control, and any PJLink projector. The app uses whichever the projector answers on, so a PJLink-only projector is an ordinary supported device.

Is Projector Pilot a subscription?

No. Basic control is free, and Pro, Home Automation and the bundle are one-time purchases. No subscription, no ads.

Does Projector Pilot need an account or the cloud?

No account and no cloud. The app talks to your projector directly on your own network.
