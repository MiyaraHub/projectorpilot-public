FAQ

Questions, answered

  • Which app do I need? Projector Pilot is for Epson projectors over ESC/VP21 and Web Control, and for any PJLink projector. Projector Pilot D-ILA is for JVC D-ILA projectors.
  • Do the apps need an account or a cloud service? No. Both apps talk to the projector on your own local network.
  • Are they subscriptions? No. Each app has a free tier, and its Pro, Home Automation and bundle purchases are one-time.
