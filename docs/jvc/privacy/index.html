Privacy

Privacy

What Projector Pilot D-ILA sends, and where. The MiyaraHub Technologies privacy policy at miyarahub.com/privacy.html is the full policy.

Data

What leaves your device

  • No account and no ads.
  • Your projector: commands go to projectors on your local network.
  • The store, for purchases and restore.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), on Android and iOS only. On by default, with a switch in Settings, About. Message text is redacted of addresses, host names, MAC addresses, URLs and tokens; the stack trace holds code locations.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase) are off until you accept them, and never include your projector's name, address, MAC or password, or any Home Assistant, Hubitat, token or trigger address.
  • With Home Automation only, and never from the demo projector: requests to the trigger addresses you enter, to Hubitat and to Home Assistant.
  • The projector password stays in the device's secure storage, on this device only, and is not included in backups.
