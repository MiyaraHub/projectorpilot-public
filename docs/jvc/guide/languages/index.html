Languages

29 languages, your choice

Projector Pilot D-ILA speaks 29 languages. It follows your device's language by default, and you can pick another one at any time without changing the rest of your device.

Change it

Pick a language

  1. Open Settings, then Language.
  2. Choose System default to follow the device, or pick a language. Each one is listed in its own name, so you can find yours even if the app is in a language you do not read.
  3. The app switches at once. No restart, no download.
A language you pick sticks until you change it, even if you change the device language later. Choose System default to follow the device again.

Right to left

Arabic and Hebrew

In Arabic and Hebrew the whole app is laid out right to left: navigation, lists and text all flow the way you read. The lens pad is the one exception, because shift left has to move the picture left whatever language the app is in, so it stays left to right.

What stays the same

What is not translated

  • Your names. Projector names and scene names are yours and stay as you typed them.
  • Maintenance reminder notifications are always in English.
  • The projector's own on-screen menu has its own language. Change it with the Language setting on the Display page of Picture, on models that support it; the app language does not change it.
Questions

Language questions

Does the language affect what the projector can do?

No. Every function, free or paid, is the same in every language. Only the words change.

Can the app and my phone use different languages?

Yes. Pick a language in Settings, Language, and only the app changes. The rest of your device stays in its own language.

Is the choice included in a backup?

Backup & Restore covers app settings; see Settings for what a backup holds and what it never holds.

This website

The guide in your language

This website is offered in the same languages. Use the language menu at the top of any page to switch.

On the projector

The projector's own menu

The menus the projector draws on your screen have their own language, set on the projector. On models that support it, change it from the app with the Language setting on the Display page of Picture. Changing the app language never changes the projector, and the projector's menu language never changes the app.

Help us

Spotted a wrong word?

If a word reads oddly in your language, tell us with Send Feedback or Report a Bug in Settings, or on Discord. Say which language and which screen, and what it should say.
