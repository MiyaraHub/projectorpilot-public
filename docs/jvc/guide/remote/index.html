Remote

The handset, on your phone

Drive the projector's own menus and reach the common picture settings without them.

Remote page

Navigate and adjust

  • Menu navigation: the d-pad, select and back, as on the JVC handset.
  • Picture shortcuts: picture mode, content type, HDR level, gamma, color temperature, color profile and motion.
  • Hide to blank the picture, menu shortcuts and the test pattern.
Free d-pad presses. The free tier includes 20 lifetime d-pad presses, then 3 per day. Pro removes the limit. The count survives a reinstall.
