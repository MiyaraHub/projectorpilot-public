Before you start

What you need

  • A JVC D-ILA projector with a LAN port, connected to your network by cable. The help page lists the model families the app knows.
  • A phone, tablet or computer on the same local network. The app works over the local network only; it has no cloud service to go through.
  • The projector's network password, if one is set on the projector. Newer models use 8 to 10 characters.
  • No projector yet? The demo projector, simulated on your device, opens every screen. See Getting started.
Free and paid

What is free

Power, HDMI 1 and a navigation allowance are free on every projector. Pro unlocks the rest of the projector; Home Automation unlocks the links to your smart home. Both are one-time purchases.

Free

Power on and standby, HDMI 1, and 20 navigation presses for the life of the install, then 3 a day.

Pro

Every input and remote key, picture, lens, installation, scenes, maintenance, test pattern and home-screen widgets.

Home Automation

Webhook triggers, the Hubitat mirror and the Home Assistant link, including its dashboard.

The full breakdown is on the pricing page.

Per model

Why a control can be missing

  • The model does not document it. A setting JVC's command table does not list for your model is not shown, rather than sent blind.
  • The projector does not answer it. A setting the projector keeps not answering while it is on is shown disabled, with "This projector does not answer this setting."
  • Another setting holds it. Some settings only apply in certain conditions, such as HDR Processing with an HDR signal. The control says which setting is holding it.
  • The projector is in standby. Most settings appear once the projector is on.
Support is discovered from the projector itself, so wording such as "on models that support it" throughout this guide means exactly that: the app checks your model, then shows what applies.
