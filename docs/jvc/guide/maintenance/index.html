Maintenance

Maintenance and insights

Know how hard the light source has worked. The Maintenance screen reads the hours from the projector, works out projected life and running cost from figures you enter, and can remind you as the rated life gets close. It is part of Pro, under Settings, Maintenance & Insights.

Hours

Read-only, from the projector

  • Light source hours. The total hours the laser or lamp has run, read from the projector. The label follows the light source your model reports.
  • Since added. The hours run since you marked this projector installed, once you set a baseline below.
  • No hours yet? Connect and power on the projector so the app can read them.
Nothing on this screen is written back to the projector. The app does not reset or change the projector's own hour count.
Insights

Projected life and cost per hour

The app does not know a rated life for any model, so it never invents one. Enter the figures from your projector's documentation or your dealer, and the insights fill in.

InsightWhat you enterWhat it shows
Projected lifeRated life, in hours, for example 20000.Hours left of the rated life and the percentage used.
Cost per hourThe cost of a replacement light source or unit, for example 3500.The cost per hour of running. No currency symbol is added, so it works in any currency.
Installed baselineMark now, or edit the hours by hand. Clear removes it.The Since added figure above.
Reminders

Maintenance reminders

Reminders are off until you opt in. Turn on Maintenance notifications, then the Light source life warning.

  • When. At 90% of the rated life you entered, then every 100 hours.
  • How. A notification titled Light source hours. The check runs while the app is open and connected to the projector.
  • Needs. A rated life under Insights, and permission for notifications. If the system refuses notifications, reminders stay off until you allow them.
  • Where. Android, iOS and macOS. Windows does not get reminders.
The reminder text is in English whatever language the app is set to.
Records

Export an hour report

Export hour report saves a small text file: the app version, the model, the light source hours the app read, the time, and a SHA-256 checksum.

It is a snapshot of what the app read at that moment, handy as a record of the projector's use. It is self-attested by the app, not certified by JVC.

Your data

Where the figures live

  • Rated life, unit cost and baseline are kept on this device.
  • Backup & Restore does not include them.
  • The hours themselves always come from the projector, so they are correct on any device you connect from.
  • The dashboard Light source hours chip and the Projector Status widget show the same reading; see Dashboard.
Example

A worked example

Say your projector has run 4000 hours, and you enter a rated life of 20000 hours and a replacement cost of 3500.

Hours left16000Rated life minus the hours run.

Cost per hour falls the longer the light source runs, because the same cost is spread over more hours.

At a glance

Questions

Maintenance questions

Why does the app not fill in the rated life for me?

The app has no rated life for any model, and it never invents one. Enter the figure from your projector's documentation or your dealer.

Can the app reset the projector's hour count?

No. Nothing on this screen is written to the projector. The installed baseline is the app's own way to count hours since a date you choose.

Do reminders work on Windows?

No. Reminders work on Android, iOS and macOS, while the app is open and connected to the projector.
