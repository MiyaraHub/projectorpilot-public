Settings

Settings

Your projectors, your purchases, and how the app looks and behaves. This page walks through the Settings tab from top to bottom.

Top of the page

Projectors and purchases

  • Projectors. Every saved projector, with its address and port. Tap one to open its Device Info; the menu beside it holds Rename, Edit connection, Change password and Remove. Add projector is here too, and Close the demo when the demo projector is open.
  • Pro card. What you own, with Restore to bring back a purchase on this store account and Refresh to check again. See Pricing.
  • Maintenance & Insights. Light source hours, projected life and reminders. See Maintenance.
  • Tools: Test Pattern. Show the projector's built-in test pattern (Pro).
Tools

Test Pattern

Test Pattern turns the projector's built-in pattern on and off, on models that offer one over the network. It is a switch, not a pattern picker.

If the projector does not report whether the pattern is showing, the button says so and offers to show it. If the projector does not take the change, the app says "The projector did not accept the test pattern." rather than pretending it did.

Appearance

Theme and display size

OptionChoices
ThemeLight, Dark, or AMOLED (true black for OLED screens).
Color ThemeThe accent that tints buttons, sliders and headers: Brand keeps the signature indigo, or pick a preset such as Forest, Ocean, Sunset, Amethyst, Slate or Crimson, or your own color by hex code. Any accent works on any theme.
Display sizeSmall, Medium, Large or Extra large.

On desktop, the window remembers its size and position.

Navigation

Make the app yours

  • Customize Navigation reorders the bottom tabs. Dashboard and Settings stay in place.
  • Customize Views hides the views inside a tab you never open, such as the Network page of the Remote tab. The first view of each tab always stays.
  • Language picks the app language. See Languages.
  • Home Automation opens the hub for Home Assistant, Hubitat and webhooks. See Home automation.
Data

Backup & Restore

Export settings writes your app settings, such as theme and display size, to a file. Import settings restores them after a reinstall or on another device.

Never in a backup: your projectors, their passwords, scenes, maintenance figures and purchases. Add projectors again after a restore, export scenes as files, and use Restore on the Pro card for purchases.

A file from a newer version of the app, from a different app, or with a value the app cannot use is refused with nothing changed.

Data

Reset All App Data

Wipes every setting and every saved projector, including their passwords, after one confirmation. It cannot be undone. Your purchases and the free navigation allowance are kept, so a reset does not restart the free presses.

Help

Help and feedback

  • Send Feedback or Report a Bug. Write a message; the report attaches the app version, your device and OS, the names and models of your saved projectors, and the in-app diagnostic log. Email support or share the report.
  • Welcome & Setup Guide. Replays the welcome screens and setup tips.
  • Guided Setup. Walks through finding and reaching your projector, with the connection checks in Getting started.
  • Links. Website, Discord, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, plus Rate the app.
About

Version, privacy switches and models

  • Version and build number, the open source licenses, and Share App.
  • Share anonymous usage analytics. Off unless you turn it on.
  • Send crash reports. On by default on Android and iOS; turn it off any time.
  • Supported Projectors. Every model the app knows, with its level: Every documented function; Power, input and remote keys; No network control; or Support level unknown. A D-ILA model not listed still gets power, input and the remote keys.
For support

Diagnostics

Tapping Version seven times in About opens the Developer Menu. Its diagnostics are read-only checks: connection timing, your projector's capabilities and a protocol probe. Export report and Copy JSON leave out the address, port, MAC address and password, so a report is safe to send to support.
