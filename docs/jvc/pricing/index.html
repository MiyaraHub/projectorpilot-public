Free

What free really means

Free is not a trial that runs out. Power and HDMI 1 work forever, and the menu keys keep working every day.

  • Always free: turning the projector on and to standby, and switching to HDMI 1.
  • Counted keys: Up, Down, Left, Right, OK, Back and Menu. You get 20 presses for the life of the app, then 3 per day.
  • The day starts at your local midnight. If you use the last of the 20 today, today's 3 still apply, so the first day can reach 23.
  • Only sent presses count. A press is not counted when the app is not connected, on the demo projector, or once you own Pro.
Pro

What Pro adds

Full remote

Every remote key without a count, every input, aspect and Hide. See Remote.

Picture

Picture mode, content type, HDR, color, ISF, motion and the rest of your model's picture settings. See Picture.

Lens and installation

Lens shift, focus and zoom, lens memories and installation modes. See Lens.

Scenes

Save a look and bring it back in one tap. See Scenes.

Maintenance

Light source hours, projected life and reminders. See Maintenance.

Widgets and more

Home screen widgets on Android and iOS, device info, menus, operation settings and the test pattern.

Each control appears on models that support it. Pro unlocks what your projector offers; it does not add functions your model does not have.

Home Automation

A separate add-on

Home Automation is sold on its own, so you can have it without Pro, and Pro on its own does not include it. It unlocks webhook triggers, the Hubitat mirror and the Home Assistant setup and dashboard. See Home automation. The bundle is both, for less than buying them apart.

Try first

The demo projector

Tap Explore Demo Mode when you first open the app to try every page, with everything unlocked and nothing sent anywhere. It costs nothing and uses none of the free presses. See Getting started.

Your purchase

Restoring and new devices

  • A purchase belongs to your store account. On a new device signed in to the same account, tap Restore on the Pro card in Settings.
  • Refresh checks the store again if a purchase does not show at once.
  • Reset All App Data keeps your purchases. A settings backup never holds them; Restore brings them back.
