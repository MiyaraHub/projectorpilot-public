What it does

The controls your D-ILA projector offers, on your phone.

Each model gets the functions its own JVC command table documents. The app shows what your projector supports and leaves out what it does not.

Power and inputs

Power on and standby, HDMI 1 and HDMI 2, with the projector's warm-up shown as it happens.

The remote

Menu navigation, picture mode, content type, HDR level, gamma, color temperature, color profile, motion, hide and menu shortcuts.

Lens and installation

Lens aperture, shift, focus and zoom with hold-to-drive, lens memories, installation modes, aspect and anamorphic.

Scenes

Save a set of settings and apply it in one tap. Share a scene as a .ppscene file; importing keeps what the model offers.

Maintenance

Light source hours against rated life, cost per hour, opt-in reminders and an hour report you can export.

Home automation

Webhooks on power, input and scene events, Hubitat through Maker API, and Home Assistant with a ready dashboard.

Supported projectors

From DLA-X to the 2025 NZ series

  • 43 model profiles covering 64 DLA model names, 52 of them network-controllable.
  • 2018 NX series: DLA-NX5, NX7, NX9 and their RS and regional names.
  • 2021 NZ series: DLA-NZ7, NZ8, NZ9 and DLA-NP5, with their RS and regional names.
  • 2024 and 2025 NZ series: DLA-NZ500, NZ700, NZ800 and NZ900.
  • Earlier LAN models such as DLA-X7, X9, X30, X70R and X90R.
  • An unlisted D-ILA projector still gets power, input and the remote keys.
FAQ

Questions, answered

  • Which JVC projectors does Projector Pilot D-ILA support? JVC D-ILA projectors with network control, from the DLA-X and DLA-RS models with a LAN port through the 2018 NX series and the 2021, 2024 and 2025 NZ series. The app carries 43 model profiles covering 64 DLA model names, 52 of them network-controllable. A D-ILA projector that is not in the list still gets power, input and the remote keys.
  • Do I need an account or a cloud service? No. The app talks to the projector directly on your local network over TCP port 20554, using the projector's own network password. There is no account and no cloud service.
  • Is it a subscription? No. Power, HDMI 1 and a number of remote presses are free. Pro, the Home Automation add-on and the bundle are one-time purchases.
  • When can I download it? Projector Pilot D-ILA is coming soon to Google Play, the App Store and the Microsoft Store. It is not on any store yet.
