Help & FAQ

Connecting, supported models, purchases and privacy.

FAQ

Common questions

  • The app cannot find or connect to my projector Put the phone and the projector on the same network and subnet; guest networks and isolated VLANs keep them apart. The scan looks at your own /24 subnet. If it does not turn the projector up, add it by IP address. Network control uses TCP port 20554, and newer models need the network password set on the projector.
  • Which password does the app ask for? The network password configured on the projector itself. The app keeps it in the device's secure storage, on this device only, and leaves it out of backups.
  • My projector changed IP address After three failed attempts the app looks for the projector on your network by its MAC address and reconnects to its new address.
  • My model is not listed An unlisted D-ILA projector still gets power, input and the remote keys. Settings that a model's command table does not offer are not shown, rather than sent blind.
  • Is it a one-time purchase? Yes. Pro, Home Automation and the bundle are one-time purchases with no subscription and no ads. The store shows the price in your own currency before you confirm.
  • Does the app collect data? Crash reports on Android and iOS are on by default and can be turned off in Settings, About. Usage analytics are off until you accept them, and never include your projector's name, address, MAC or password.
Supported models

Model families

  • Earlier models with LAN: DLA-X7, X9, RS50, RS60, X30, RS45, X70R, X90R, RS55 and RS65.
  • 2018: DLA-NX5, NX7, NX9 and the RS1000, RS2000, RS3000, N5, N6, N8, N11, V5, V7 and V9R names.
  • 2021: DLA-NZ7, NZ8, NZ9, NP5 and the RS1100, RS2100, RS3100, RS4100, N88, N98, V50, V70R, V80R and V90R names.
  • 2024: DLA-NZ500, NZ700 and the RS1200, RS2200, N700, N788, N799, N800, N888, N899, Z5 and Z7 names.
  • 2025: DLA-NZ800 and NZ900.
  • Earlier models without LAN, such as DLA-HD350, HD550, HD750, HD950, HD990, X3, RS10, RS15, RS20, RS25, RS35 and RS40, cannot be controlled over the network.
