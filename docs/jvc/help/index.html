Help

Help & FAQ

Connecting, staying connected, supported models, purchases and privacy. Most connection problems come down to three things: the same network, the projector in standby with its LAN cable connected, and no other controller holding the projector.

Start here

A quick checklist

  1. The projector is on, or in standby with its LAN cable connected.
  2. The phone, tablet or computer is on the same network and subnet as the projector, not a guest network.
  3. Nothing else is connected to the projector: Home Assistant, a control system or another copy of this app.
  4. If the projector has a network password, you have it to hand.
  5. Run Guided Setup from Settings. It checks each of these and says which one failed.
FAQ

Common questions

The app cannot find or connect to my projector

Put the phone and the projector on the same network and subnet; guest networks, isolated VLANs and client isolation keep them apart. The scan looks at your own /24 subnet. If it does not turn the projector up, add it by IP address. Network control uses TCP port 20554, and newer models need the network password set on the projector.

Which password does the app ask for?

The network password configured on the projector itself, not a Wi-Fi password. Newer models use 8 to 10 characters. The app keeps it in the device's secure storage, on this device only, and leaves it out of backups.

The projector is in standby and the app cannot reach it

A projector that is powered fully off cannot answer. Keep it in standby with its LAN cable connected. On some models an energy saving standby setting such as Eco Mode stops the network from answering in standby; check the projector's own network and standby settings.

It says another controller is connected

A JVC projector serves one network controller at a time. If Home Assistant, a control system or another app is connected, this app cannot connect until it lets go, and the reverse is also true. This app lets go of the projector when it goes to the background.

My projector changed IP address

After three failed attempts in a row the app looks for the projector on your network by its MAC address and reconnects to its new address. It needs to have connected once before, so it knows the MAC address. You can also change the address with Edit connection in Settings.

My model is not listed

An unlisted D-ILA projector still gets power, input and the remote keys. Settings that a model's command table does not offer are not shown, rather than sent blind. Settings, About, Supported Projectors lists every model the app knows and its support level.

Why is a control missing on my projector?

The app shows a control only when your model's command table lists it and the projector answers for it. Controls also wait while the projector is in standby or warming up, and appear once it is on.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Pro, Home Automation and the bundle are one-time purchases with no subscription and no ads. The store shows the price in your own currency before you confirm.

How do I get my purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the same store account and tap Restore on the Pro card in Settings. A purchase belongs to the store account, not to the device. If nothing is found, the app says so.

Does the app collect data?

Crash reports on Android and iOS are on by default and can be turned off in Settings, About. Usage analytics are off until you accept them, and never include your projector's name, address, MAC or password.

Has the app been tested on a real JVC projector?

Yes, on a DLA-NZ500: connecting with the network password, reading its state and changing settings. Other models are supported from JVC's published command tables, and the app only offers what each model's table lists.

Troubleshooting

What the connection check tells you

Guided Setup and the add screen check the address you give and name the first thing that failed. Each result comes with what to do next.

ResultWhat it meansWhat to do
Different networkThe phone and the projector are on different subnets.Put the phone on the same network as the projector, not a guest network, or allow traffic between VLANs.
No device at that addressNothing answered at that address.Check the IP in the projector's network settings, and that it is in standby with its LAN cable connected.
Control port refusedSomething is at the address but refused the control port.Check the port. JVC D-ILA projectors use 20554 by default.
No reply on the control portThe port timed out with no answer.The projector may be fully off, another controller may be connected, or the network may block device-to-device traffic (client or AP isolation).
Password neededThe projector asks for a network password.Enter the network password set on the projector.
Password not acceptedThe projector refused the password.Check the password on the projector and enter it again.
Projector busyAnother controller is connected.Close other apps or systems that control the projector, wait a few seconds and check again.
Not a JVC replySomething answered but did not complete a JVC handshake.Check the address: the port may belong to a different device.
While connected

Messages you may see

  • Nothing answered at this address on port 20554. The projector is off the network or at another address.
  • The projector did not accept a connection on port 20554. Either it is off the network, or another controller such as Home Assistant is connected to it.
  • The projector closed the connection. The projector ended the session; the app reconnects on its own.
  • The saved password could not be read from secure storage. Set it again with Change password in Settings.
Supported models

Model families

The app knows 43 model profiles. Each name below is a model or a regional name for one.

  • Earlier models with LAN: DLA-X7, X9, RS50, RS60, X30, RS45, X70R, X90R, RS55 and RS65.
  • 2018: DLA-NX5, NX7, NX9 and the RS1000, RS2000, RS3000, N5, N6, N8, N11, V5, V7 and V9R names.
  • 2021: DLA-NZ7, NZ8, NZ9, NP5 and the RS1100, RS2100, RS3100, RS4100, N88, N98, V50, V70R, V80R and V90R names.
  • 2024: DLA-NZ500, NZ700 and the RS1200, RS2200, N700, N788, N799, N800, N888, N899, Z5 and Z7 names.
  • 2025: DLA-NZ800 and NZ900.
  • Earlier models without LAN, such as DLA-HD350, HD550, HD750, HD950, HD990, X3, RS10, RS15, RS20, RS25, RS35 and RS40, cannot be controlled over the network.
Reporting a problem

What to send support

  • Send Feedback or Report a Bug in Settings attaches the app version, your device and OS, the names and models of your saved projectors, and the in-app diagnostic log.
  • The Developer Menu's diagnostics report leaves out the address, port, MAC address and password, so it is safe to share.
  • Say which model you have and what you tapped. A message the app showed, copied word for word, helps most.
